## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a digital sanctuary for Nicaraguan cultural education, folklore, and heritage craftsmanship. The brand personality balances artisanal gravitas with contemporary editorial grace: grounded, dignified, sensory, and deeply narrative-driven. 

The aesthetic marries **Tactile Modern Editorial** with refined cultural heritage. Drawing inspiration from mother-of-pearl ("nácar"), volcanic clays, woven jícara vessels, and traditional colonial textiles, the UI favors layered parchment-warm planes, crisp typographical hierarchy, high-contrast cultural photography, and tailored interactive surfaces. The visual rhythm avoids sterile software ergonomics in favor of an immersive monograph feel, inviting learners and creators to explore heritage arts with pride, intellectual curiosity, and aesthetic wonder.

## Colors

The palette balances intense artisanal pigmentation with warm, tactile neutrals:

- **Primary Wine / Deep Garnet (`#73020C`):** Represents ceremonial heritage, fermented cacao, and classical textile dyes. Used for key call-to-actions, brand statements, primary icons, and active focal elements.
- **Crimson Accent (`#BF1131`):** A lively vermilion spark inspired by indigenous festive garb and hibiscus blooms. Utilized for badges, dynamic hover states, urgent alerts, progress indicators, and interactive highlights.
- **Serene Cerulean Blue (`#6BA7BF`):** A tranquil atmospheric counterweight reminiscent of Lake Cocibolca and Pacific coastal skies. Serves as a tertiary accent for course tags, reflective states, subtle secondary highlights, and complimentary data graphics.
- **Deep Cocoa / Espresso (`#260101`):** The primary neutral text tone; richer and softer than mechanical pure black, anchoring headlines and running copy in warm ink.
- **Warm Pearl Cream / Sand (`#F2E2CE`):** The foundational canvas tint representing untamed linen, sand, and polished mother-of-pearl. Provides natural warmth behind pure white (`#FFFFFF`) and warm white (`#FAF6F0`) floating cards and panels.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs the high-contrast elegance of **Playfair Display** with the approachable clarity of **Plus Jakarta Sans**. 

- **Display & Headlines:** Playfair Display introduces literary poise, traditional craft dignity, and editorial presence. Its sharp transitions and calligraphic terminals echo traditional woodcut and bookmaking arts. For mobile breakpoints, display styles step down automatically to maintain comfortable density and prevent excessive line breaks.
- **Body & UI Roles:** Plus Jakarta Sans provides contemporary readability, open apertures, and warm curves that mirror the rounded geometry of handcrafted pottery. It anchors long-form educational prose, lesson step-by-step guides, course navigation, and transactional modules without competing with editorial headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system employs a 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by comfortable exterior gutters and generous section margins to honor the spaciousness of cultural exhibition spaces:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns with `1.5rem` gutters and a maximum content wrapper of `1320px`. Outer margins are fixed at `3rem` to establish clear breathing room from browser boundaries.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns with `1.25rem` gutters and `2rem` outer margins. Multi-column editorial modules collapse gracefully into balanced pairs.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` margins. Complex module sidebars fold into sticky bottom bars or drawer trays.
- **Vertical Spacing Rhythm:** Built upon an 8px base grid. Layout components maintain substantial vertical margins (`2.5rem` to `4rem`) between distinct narrative chapters and lesson segments to ensure content breathes naturally.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through layered organic planes and tinted warm shadows rather than clinical grays:

- **Surface Tiers:** 
  1. *Canvas Base:* Tinted warm pearl cream (`#F2E2CE`).
  2. *Elevated Content Containers:* Clean warm white (`#FAF6F0`) or pristine white (`#FFFFFF`) planes.
  3. *Overlays & Floating Sheets:* Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with intentional crimson or garnet accent trims.
- **Ambient Shadow Craft:** Shadows are warmly tinted using the Deep Cocoa neutral (`#260101` at 5% to 12% opacity), avoiding synthetic neutral grays:
  - *Resting Card Elevation:* `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(38, 1, 1, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(38, 1, 1, 0.04)`
  - *Interactive / Hover Elevation:* `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(38, 1, 1, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(38, 1, 1, 0.06)`
- **Low-Contrast Framing:** Containers utilize a 1px border rendered in cocoa at 8% opacity (`rgba(38, 1, 1, 0.08)`) to preserve tactile shape definition when layered against light backgrounds.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a balanced roundedness (`roundedness: 2` = 0.5rem base radius) evoking the smoothed, burnished contours of Nicaraguan earthenware and river-polished stone. 

- **Standard Elements (0.5rem / 8px):** Input inputs, standard buttons, interactive lesson pills, and dropdown sheets.
- **Container Surfaces (`rounded-lg`, 1rem / 16px):** Course overview cards, instructional panels, video containers, and artisan profile modules.
- **Hero & Dialog Surfaces (`rounded-xl`, 1.5rem / 24px):** Modals, featured masterclass highlights, and showcase banners.
- **Full Radii (`rounded-full`):** Category tags, audio playtime capsules, and user avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Primary Wine (`#73020C`) with pure white text and an 8px border radius. On hover, shifts to Crimson Accent (`#BF1131`) with a subtle lift shadow.
- **Secondary:** Filled in warm white (`#FAF6F0`) with a 1.5px border of Primary Wine (`#73020C`) and wine-colored label text.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background with Cerulean Blue (`#6BA7BF`) or Deep Cocoa (`#260101`) text and an underline transition on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Curatorial Tags:** Rendered in Serene Cerulean Blue tint (`rgba(107, 167, 191, 0.15)`) with Cerulean text (`#457A90`), fully rounded (`rounded-full`), displaying craft technique or regional origin (e.g., *San Juan de Oriente*, *Masaya*).
- **Status Badges:** Crimson Accent (`#BF1131`) pill with white text for active live workshops, certifications, or new masterclasses.

### Cards
- **Course & Craft Story Cards:** Crafted on warm white (`#FAF6F0`) surfaces with a 16px radius (`rounded-lg`) and 1px cocoa perimeter border (`rgba(38, 1, 1, 0.08)`). Imagery occupies the top half with a natural 4:3 or 16:9 ratio, paired with Playfair Display titles and Plus Jakarta Sans meta information below.
- **Hover State:** Rises smoothly by -4px with an elevated cocoa-tinted shadow.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Text Fields:** Background in pure white (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px border in cocoa (`rgba(38, 1, 1, 0.18)`), 8px border radius, and label rendered in Plus Jakarta Sans SemiBold. Focused inputs transition to a 1.5px border in Primary Wine (`#73020C`) with a soft wine glow (`rgba(115, 2, 12, 0.1)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Primary Wine fills with crisp white checkmarks and circular dots. The unfocused boundary uses cocoa at 30% opacity.

### Lists & Navigation
- **Module Curriculum List:** Alternating or bordered list rows with 12px internal vertical padding, separated by fine lines (`rgba(38, 1, 1, 0.06)`). Completed lessons display a Crimson Accent checkmark inside a Cerulean-tinted circular badge.
- **Navigation Bar:** Elevated cream sand or warm white surface with a bottom border separator and rich wine typography for active navigation anchors.

### Cultural Showcase Components
- **Artisan Quote / Story Inset:** Warm sand backdrop with an editorial Playfair Display italic excerpt, accented with a 4px solid Primary Wine left decorative line.
- **Audio & Media Player Capsule:** Fully rounded player pill housing Cerulean waveform progress bars, Deep Cocoa playback controls, and a Crimson scrub handle.
